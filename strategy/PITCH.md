# SlayKesh — 3 Ideas to Grow Revenue & Repeat (prospect pitch)

*Built from a public audit of slaykesh.com, 12 competitors and Indian audience research. Budgets and outcomes are directional estimates, to be firmed up with SlayKesh's real numbers.*

## What we noticed (in 60 seconds)
- **Your best asset isn't on your shelf, it's your story.** Nearly losing your hair at 20 after becoming a mother at 19. No competitor has a founder who has lived the problem. It sits on the About page, but it could run the whole brand.
- **You're leaking money on the first order.** The ₹749 serum and the ₹899 mask + comb both sit just under the ₹999 free-shipping line.
- **Nothing brings a customer back.** There is no subscribe-and-save and no refill reminder, even though a serum runs out in about 30 days.
- **The hero product has three names** (Scalp Nurture, Root Revival, Grow From Root), and another seller already uses "Root Revival".
- **The neem comb is an unclaimed hero.** No brand at ₹600–900 sells a scalp *tool* as part of a routine.

---

## Idea 1 — "Maine bhi jhela hai" · The Mom Circle
**Own postpartum hair fall, through Suhasini.**
- 4–5 phone-shot "Suhasini's diary" Reels a week: *"Delivery ke 3 mahine baad bathroom mein itne baal? Main 20 ki thi jab mere saath ye hua."*
- A **WhatsApp Mom Circle**: a weekly founder voice note, and refills timed to the baby's age (months 3, 4 and 6). Repeat purchases follow a biological clock, not a coupon calendar.
- Rename the ₹1,399 kit the **"New Mom Scalp Kit"**, a baby-shower gift, so the sister or best friend buys it.
- YouTube: long explainers that rank for "hair fall after delivery".
- **Cost:** lowest of the three, around ₹25–60K a month (estimated). Mostly an editor, a WhatsApp tool and barter with mom creators.
- **Catch:** needs about 6–8 founder hours a week, and a doctor's sign-off on breastfeeding safety before launch.

## Idea 2 — "Scalp first. Slay follows." · The 30-Day Scalp Reset
**Turn three products into one ritual for women aged 18–26 (hostel water, exam stress).**
- A **"Ritual Starter" (serum + neem comb) at exactly ₹999**. It crosses free shipping and lifts AOV.
- A **30-Day Scalp Reset** challenge run by nano creators on commission (pay per sale, not per post). It ends on day 25, exactly when the serum runs out, which is when the refill nudge fires.
- *"₹25 a day. Teri chai se sasta."* · 60-second neem-comb ASMR Shorts.
- **Cost:** around ₹50–120K a month (estimated), most of it only paid when sales happen.
- **Catch:** more exposed to Minimalist and WishCare on price.

## Idea 3 — "Proof, har 30 din" · The 90-Day Plan on WhatsApp
**A coached ₹2,799 plan, less than half of Traya's monthly cost, sold entirely in WhatsApp.**
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a short scalp quiz, then an (automated) scalp coach, then a 90-day plan paid upfront.
- Progress photos every 30 days show the proof. That builds in repeat, since customers commit for 90 days.
- For women with stress-related hair fall who have "tried everything". Positioned honestly as scalp support alongside their doctor, never as a PCOS treatment.
- **Cost:** highest, around ₹1–2.5L a month (estimated) including ads. Start lean and scale only once cost per customer is proven.
- **Catch:** most cash up front, on Traya's home ground.

---

## Quick wins we'd ship in week 1 (any idea)
1. One product name everywhere: **Scalp Nurture**.
2. A ₹999 bundle so the first order crosses free shipping.
3. Subscribe & save on the serum, plus a day-25 WhatsApp refill reminder.
4. Reframe the "60-day hair growth" claim. Visible results take 90+ days, and over-promising causes churn.
5. Tidy the marketplace listings (an off-brand hair-clip listing and mismatched names).

**Our recommendation to start:** use Idea 1 as the brand, run Idea 2's ₹999 ritual and refill flow as the money engine, and keep Idea 3 as the scale-up once the numbers are proven.
